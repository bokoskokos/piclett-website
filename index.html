How to play

Just like dominoes, but every card is a picture, a letter or a word.

1

Match

Find a card whose first letter matches the open end of the chain. A picture of a ball matches the letter B.

2

Draw

Nothing fits? Draw a new card from the deck and keep the game moving.

3

Win

Play against the computer. The first player to use all their cards wins the game.
