Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Piclett is designed for children and families. We respect your privacy and have designed Piclett to work without collecting personal information.

Information we collect

Piclett does not collect, store or transmit personal information. Piclett does not require an account or registration.

Data collection

Piclett does not collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Photos
  • Contacts
  • Location information
  • Device identifiers for tracking
  • Usage analytics
  • Advertising identifiers

Piclett does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Information stored on your device

Piclett may save simple settings on your device, such as whether you have already seen the tutorial. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Internet connection

Piclett is designed to work offline. The game does not require an internet connection to play.

Children

Piclett is designed for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Purchases

Piclett is available as a one-time purchase through the Apple App Store. Apple processes purchases according to Apple's own privacy policies and terms. We do not receive your payment details.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy or Piclett, contact us at support@piclett.com.
