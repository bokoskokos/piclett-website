Support

We're happy to help.

Contact us

Having trouble with Piclett or have a question? Email us at support@piclett.com. We aim to respond to support requests as soon as possible.

Does Piclett need an internet connection?

No. Piclett works completely offline.

Does Piclett collect any information?

No. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. Read the privacy policy for details.

How do I play?

Match the open end of the chain with a card whose picture, letter or word starts with the same letter. If you can't play, draw a card from the deck. The first player to use all their cards wins.

Purchases and refunds

Piclett is a one-time purchase with no subscription. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
